# V9 bracket V2 with four 0p15 channels

Stage: `v9-bracket-v2`. Previous recorded stage: `v9c-fit`. Retrospectively documented 2026-09-20.

## Change and reason

Applied tested 0.15 mm fit to all four brace channels; X, spacing, top and supports unchanged. No anti-lift feature. About 0.53 g solid PLA added over prior brace.

## Recorded CAD/analysis evidence

Recorded one watertight component, A1 envelope, nominal assembly/removal/top-install checks. Regression against old brace plus four tighter coupons differed 0.00994 mm³ within documented 0.02 mm³ numerical tolerance.

## User-reported physical evidence

User printed V2: more stable, but during left/right racking the upper channels again slid up off the T rails. User emphasized that the brace still does not prevent racking.

## Outcome and next question

FAILED full-frame racking/retention despite improved local fit. Further tightening is not the established fix. Open question: when does vertical channel motion begin relative to frame racking? A proposed anti-lift feature must carry load and constrain racking, not merely catch a detached brace.
